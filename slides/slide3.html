Cancer

Uncontrolled cell growth from genetic alterations, affecting proto-oncogenes, tumor suppressor genes & DNA repair pathways

G12C Mutation

Glycine → Cysteine substitution at codon 12 — impairs GTP hydrolysis, locks KRAS in permanently active state

Lung Cancer Types

NSCLC (85%) vs SCLC (15%). Risk factors: smoking, air pollution, occupational exposure

Oncogenic Pathway

Activates MAPK/ERK and PI3K/AKT cascades → uncontrolled proliferation & tumor progression

KRAS Gene

Encodes a GTPase protein — molecular switch alternating between GTP-bound (active) and GDP-bound (inactive) states

Targeted Therapy

G12C creates a unique cysteine pocket exploited by covalent inhibitors like Adagrasib (MRTX849)
