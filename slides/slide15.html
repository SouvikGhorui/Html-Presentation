STRUCTURAL ORGANIZATION

Secondary & Tertiary Structure of KRAS G12C

α-Helices, β-Sheets and Loop regions form the functional 3D architecture

α-Helices
C-terminal region

Tightly packed coiled structures contributing to overall protein stability and shape

β-Sheets
N-terminal region

Rigid structural reinforcement through inter-strand hydrogen bonding — provides scaffold for GTP binding

Loop Regions
Throughout protein

Flexible connectors between helices & sheets — facilitate conformational changes during signaling & drug binding

Turns & Coils
Switch I & II

Switch regions undergo conformational change upon GTP/GDP exchange — key targets for KRAS inhibitors
