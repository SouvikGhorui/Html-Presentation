KRAS MOLECULAR MECHANISM

Normal vs G12C Mutant KRAS: The Oncogenic Switch

NORMAL KRAS

Healthy Cellular Signaling

GTP-bound state: ACTIVE (Signal ON)

GDP-bound state: INACTIVE (Signal OFF)

GTP hydrolysis is intact and highly regulated

Normal cell growth and proliferation

Balanced RAS effector engagement

KRAS G12C MUTANT

Constitutively Oncogenic State

Glycine → Cysteine substitution at codon 12

GTP hydrolysis is severely impaired

Locked permanently in ACTIVE state

Continuous uncontrolled proliferation

Drives NSCLC, pancreatic & colon cancer

Downstream Signaling Pathways:
MAPK / ERK PI3K / AKT RAL-GDS RhoGEFs Tiam1
