CHAPTER 5 — OBSERVATIONS & FINDINGS

Key Findings & Observations

Comprehensive results from all 8 stages of the bioinformatics analysis

⚡

BLAST Analysis

99–100% query coverage & identity with human KRAS — GTPase domain architecture preserved despite G12C mutation

🧬

MSA (Clustal Omega)

High conservation across 6USZ, 10NU, 6OIM — conserved GTP-binding motifs confirmed in all three structures

⚛️

PyMOL Visualization

N-terminal β-sheets and C-terminal α-helices identified; GDP, Mg²⁺ and QH4 ligands localized in binding pocket

📉

B-Factor Analysis

Regions 1–3 show high flexibility (72–82 Å²); GLU/ASP in surface loops; VAL in stable hydrophobic core

🛡️

InterProScan Domains

G1–G4 motifs confirmed; G12C mutation adjacent to P-loop disrupts GTP hydrolysis — constitutive activation

🌳

Phylogenetic Analysis

47-leaf primate cluster with KRAS query — strong evolutionary conservation confirms critical signaling role

SCIENTIFIC VERDICT

The integrated analysis confirms that while the primary architecture remains intact, the G12C mutation strategically alters the Switch-II pocket and flexibility dynamics, creating the unique therapeutic window exploited by modern covalent inhibitors.
